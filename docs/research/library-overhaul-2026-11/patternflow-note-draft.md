Hi SeungHun,

I'm Jeremy. I work on Luxel, an open-source LED controller with its own
pattern language (github.com/GoogleBot42/luxel). I'm writing because
Patternflow's presets inspired a set of Luxel's built-in patterns, and I want
to say so directly instead of leaving it to a header comment.

During a rework of our pattern library we read through your presets and wrote
plain-prose descriptions of the ideas behind 34 of them. A separate pass then
wrote new Luxel code from those descriptions alone. Midsummer Sea became the
starting point for our Ocean pattern, Firefly Hollow for Meadow, Contour Blobs
for Topo Map, and Glow Tendrils for Neon Veins. Breakout Arcade's power-ups
and item-chasing AI went into our brick-breaker game.

To be clear: no code and no preset data were copied. The ports reimplement
the ideas. Your presets are CC BY-SA 4.0 and our library is Apache-2.0, so we
kept to ideas only. Each of those patterns credits the preset it came from,
and library/README.md
(github.com/GoogleBot42/luxel/blob/master/library/README.md) explains the
approach.

If you'd like the credit worded differently, or would rather have a link to
Patternflow or your profile, tell me what you'd prefer and I'll change it.

Thanks for putting the work out there.

— Jeremy
