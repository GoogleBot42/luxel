Luxel library review

Every item in the built-in library as of PR #1009 (master ). Patterns: host renders at 32x32 and 64x64 (8 frames over 8 s) plus a 144 px strip waterfall (time runs down) for files that export render(index). Scenes: the wasm compositor at 64x64 and 128x128 (the 2x2 chain), 6 frames over the loop or 10 s, clock pinned to 01:46 UTC. Verdicts are saved in this browser, keyed by library path (fire, scenes/fire-clock, sprites/heart); Export notes downloads them (and copies them to the clipboard).
